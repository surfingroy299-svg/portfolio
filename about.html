Experience & Approach

I'm a product designer at Spines, where I've spent the past year as the company's sole designer, working closely with engineers across product flows, design systems, research, and internal tools. Before that, I worked for a year as a freelance designer across different product and visual projects. Earlier, I completed extended military service in an elite unit, an experience that shaped how I handle responsibility and pressure.

I'm rarely interested in staying strictly within the boundaries of a role. The most interesting problems usually sit somewhere between design, product, and technology. I care about doing good work, helping the people around me succeed, and leaving things better than I found them. Spending a year traveling the world also taught me to adapt quickly and feel comfortable in unfamiliar situations.
